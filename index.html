        How to play

        You get a locked satellite view of an airport. Name it in 5 attempts or fewer. Pick an airport from the suggestions, then press Guess.

        Each wrong guess shows the distance and compass direction from your guess to the answer, and how close you were.

        Help along the way

        • Zoom out: a wider view, once per game, costs 1 attempt.
        • Hints: continent, country, first letter or number of runways. Each costs 1 attempt.
        • Guesses, hints and zoom-outs share the same 5 attempts. The image is fixed: no panning or zooming.

        Modes

        Daily: one of the world's 50 busiest airports by passengers, the same for everyone each UTC day; every airport comes up once before any repeats. Hard mode: a separate daily drawn from every other airport, with its own results and streak. Practice: endless random airports. Autocomplete searches every airport in all modes.

        Sharing

        Shared results use colour squares by distance (green correct, yellow under 500 km, orange under 2,000, red under 5,000, black further) with an arrow toward the answer. A light bulb marks a hint and a telescope the zoom-out.

        About & credits

        About

        Airport Guesser shows a locked satellite view of an airport. Name it in 5 attempts or fewer: each wrong guess gives the distance and direction to the answer, and you can spend an attempt on a hint (continent, country, first letter, number of runways) or a wider view. The Daily airport is the same for everyone.

        Other data

        • Country names come from the OurAirports countries table; continent codes from the OurAirports airport records. Continent names are the app's own labels.
        • The Daily airport is drawn from the 50 busiest airports worldwide by total passengers: ACI World annual figures for 2025, as compiled on Wikipedia (retrieved ). The source publishes the top 50 only. ACI World
        • Wikipedia is used as a link target only: after a round ends, the result card offers a link to the airport's Wikipedia article. The app never contacts Wikipedia itself, and nothing is shown or prefetched before the round ends. Opening the link leaves the app and goes to wikipedia.org (new tab, text under CC BY-SA, see Wikipedia). The article titles were looked up at build time from OurAirports' Wikipedia links, falling back to Wikidata (CC0); airports without a verified article get a Wikipedia search link instead.
        • The Practice "Major hubs" set is ranked by route counts from OpenFlights (ODbL) routes data, used only to order airports at build time.
