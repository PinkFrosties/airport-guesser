      How to play

      Identify the airport from the satellite image. You have five guesses. Pick an airport from the suggestions, then press Guess.

      Every miss shows the distance from your guess to the answer, the compass direction toward it, and how close you were as a percentage of half the Earth's circumference.

      Free hints

      • Start: a tight view of the airfield.
      • Miss 1 and 2: the view zooms out.
      • Miss 3: the continent. Miss 4: the country.

      Modes

      Daily is the same airport for everyone, one attempt per UTC day. Practice is endless: Easy is roughly the 100 best-connected hubs, Medium all large airports, Hard medium airports with scheduled service.

      Sharing

      Shared results use colour squares by distance: green correct, yellow under 500 km, orange under 2,000 km, red under 5,000 km, black further. Arrows show the direction toward the answer.
